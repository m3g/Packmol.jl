Packing by compression

Some systems do not pack from a random placement at their density: long chains, polymers, crowded large molecules. The molecules jam before they fill the space, each blocked by its neighbors, while the same molecules pack in seconds at a lower density. The keyword use_compression packs such systems at a lower density, and compresses them from there, in steps, to the density of the input:

use_compression       # pack at half the density, then compress

The input describes the system at its density (the constraints, the periodic cell, the numbers of molecules), and use_compression [<fraction> [<step>]] takes two optional values:

  • fraction (default 0.5): the fraction of that density at which the system is first packed. With 0.5, the system is packed in twice its volume, at half the density; with 0.3, at 30% of it.
  • step (default 0.05): the initial step of the compression, the fraction by which the linear dimensions shrink at each step (5%).

use_compression alone is use_compression 0.5 0.05:

  1. The constraints and the periodic cell are expanded by fraction^(-1/3) (by 1.26 for 0.5), about the center of the cell (the origin, without periodic boundary conditions), and the system is packed there.
  2. At each step, the geometry is scaled by 1 - step about the same center: the constraints, the cell, and the positions of the free molecules, each moved as a whole (the center of mass of a molecule is scaled; a polymer moves with the center of its monomers). Fixed molecules stay where they are. The system is packed again, from there, for up to 100 loops.
  3. A step that does not converge is undone, and tried again with half the step; after two steps that converge, the step grows by 1.5, up to the initial step. The compression ends at the geometry of the input, or stops (with a warning, the output at the density reached) if a step smaller than 0.0005 does not converge.

The report shows each step:

  Compression step    1: density   58.32% of the target, not converged (4.3 s)
  Compression step    2: density   53.95% of the target, converged (0.2 s)
  ...
  Compressed to the density of the input in 20 steps (41.3 s).

The compression works with rigid molecules and with Polymers. With polymers it goes much further: the chains of the condensate of the tutorial Biomolecular condensate, packed as rigid molecules, jam at about 220 mg/mL, while released as flexible chains they are compressed to 400 mg/mL in about 40 seconds. Small steps matter: compressed in steps of 3%, without the adaptive step, they stopped at 263 mg/mL (the chains pushed into each other by a large step get threaded through each other's monomers), and in steps of 0.5% they reached 400 mg/mL in 70 steps.

In Julia, use_compression is a keyword argument of packmol and of PackmolSystem: use_compression = true (the defaults), use_compression = 0.3 (the fraction), or use_compression = (0.3, 0.02) (the fraction and the initial step).

Periodic cells and fixed molecules

All periodic cells are supported (orthorhombic, triclinic, dodecahedral, octahedral): they are scaled uniformly about their center, which keeps their shape.

Fixed molecules are supported, and are not moved: the free molecules are packed around them in the expanded geometry and compressed toward them. This is right when the free molecules are placed freely around the fixed ones (polymers or a solvent around a nanoparticle or a protein), since the expanded cell and constraints contain them.

Limitations
  • The constraints must be boxes, cubes, spheres, cylinders, ellipsoids or planes (the others cannot be scaled).
  • The constraints are scaled and the fixed molecules are not: a constraint that is placed relative to a fixed molecule loses its relation to it in the expanded geometry, and recovers it only at the end of the compression. For example, the membrane of the Zika virion (a large system), in spherical shells chosen to fit inside the fixed shell of proteins, crossed by their transmembrane segments: expanded, the shells of the membrane would be beyond the proteins, and the lipids packed there would be compressed through them. In such systems, pack the free molecules whose regions are tied to the fixed ones without use_compression.
  • Not with restart_from.
